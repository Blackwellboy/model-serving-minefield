# Evidence status

- `reproduced here`: the repository authors ran it under stated conditions and
  the published check/raw allows scrutiny.
- `contributor-measured, conditions as reported`: a contributor measured it;
  preserve their exact conditions and attribution.
- `reported by others`: credited and linked, not independently reproduced.
- `measured here, raw not published`: measured by the authors, but strangers
  cannot inspect the raw.
- `under test`: unsettled.

Compound labels retain every component. An agent may use weaker evidence to
propose a possible match and a confirmation test. It must not present that
match as universally proven.
